Enzo is a dog who believes he will become human one day.
Denny is a race car driver and Enzo's owner.
The story focuses on family, loyalty, and perseverance.
Enzo learns important lessons by observing people.
Denny spends many years racing cars and improving his skills.
Racing requires concentration and quick decisions.
Drivers must remain calm under pressure.
The family faces many challenges throughout the story.
Success often comes from patience and determination.
Enzo believes that life is similar to racing.
